Privacy Policy

Prehistoric Memories · Last updated: 2026-07-24

This policy covers the Pinterest API integration ("the app") used to publish content to the Prehistoric Memories Pinterest account. Prehistoric Memories itself is a content page (Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest) publishing science and history facts — it does not operate a website that collects visitor data, run advertising trackers, or maintain user accounts.

What the app accesses

The app connects, via Pinterest's OAuth authorization flow, to a single Pinterest account owned and operated by the app's developer. It reads and creates boards and Pins on that one account only. It does not access, read, or modify any other Pinterest user's account, boards, or Pins.

What data is collected

The app does not collect data about visitors, followers, or anyone interacting with Prehistoric Memories' social content. The only data involved is the OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Pinterest for the developer's own account, used solely to authenticate API requests. These tokens are stored in a private, non-public configuration file and are never shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party.

Third parties

The app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-broker service. No data collected through this integration is sold or shared.

Data retention and deletion

OAuth tokens are retained only as long as the app remains connected to Pinterest. Disconnecting the app from the Pinterest account (via Pinterest's own account settings) revokes the tokens immediately. The developer can also delete the stored tokens at any time by removing the local configuration file.
